- Manitoba leads Canada in gas price drops
  Motorists in Steinbach are enjoying some welcome relief at the pumps as gasoline prices fall below $1.60 per litre at some local stations. The decline is part of a broader trend across the prairies, with Manitoba recording the largest weekly decrease in gasoline prices anywhere in Canada. GasBuddy head of petroleum analysis Patrick De Haan says a combination of improving refinery operations, seasonal changes, and declining oil prices has helped bring costs down. However, he cautions that international developments could determine how long motorists continue enjoying the savings. De Haan notes that the recent reductions follow a period of unusually high prices caused by refinery disruptions across Western Canada.
